Hey! I'm Gustavo, also known as **BayerKosmo**. 👨‍💻

I'm a Systems Analysis and Development graduate from **UniCesumar**, Ponta Grossa - Paraná, Brazil, and currently pursuing a postgraduate degree in **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at PUC Minas**, Belo Horizonte - Minas Gerais, Brazil.

### 🚀 Where It All Began

My passion for technology started when I was just **6 years old**.

Back in the day, my parents had a 2003 PC sitting in our living room. It wasn't anything extraordinary, but for me, it opened the door to an entirely new universe.

I spent hours exploring games like **Club Penguin, Roblox, KoGaMa**, and many others. What started as simple curiosity and entertainment gradually became something much bigger.

At 14, I discovered the fascinating world of computer hardware, software, and programming. I started looking beyond the screen, wanting to understand how everything actually worked.

And well... the rest is history. 🌠

Today, that same curiosity drives me to explore the universe of software development, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and everything in between.
